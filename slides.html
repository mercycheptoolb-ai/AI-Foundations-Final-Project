👩🏾‍⚕️ Meet Sarah, a community health worker in Kenya

Walks to homes with a phone, a MUAC tape and the family's booklet. One doctor for every 5,000 people in Kenya.

Amani, 19 months: MUAC 12.2 (yellow), weight "on the curve". Sarah must decide alone:

❓ refer today?   ❓ losing weight?   ❓ what to tell her mother?

Sarah is illustrative, based on Kenya's CHW programme as described by Living Goods.

17.6% of Kenyan under-5s stunted · 27.6% in the poorest homes
9–37% stunting across counties · 24 counties above the national average
up to 11× higher risk of death with severe wasting (UNICEF)

The paper chart shows weight-for-age only: no wasting check, no trend alert, advice from memory.

✨ What's new: split the work

🤖 AI reads the messy note or photo, in English or Swahili the hook
🧑🏾‍⚕️ Sarah confirms every number
📐 Code decides: WHO z-scores + referral the AI can't change the trust
🤖 AI writes next steps + a message for the family, kept in line by a guard

No single piece is new (ChatGPT reads notes, WHO Anthro computes z-scores, eCHIS has forms). Having both the hook and the trust at once is. Built to plug into eCHIS (107,000 CHWs).

🛡️ Safety

3 layers: code (sanitizer, grounding, ranges) · design (WHO rules decide; guard sends back) · prompt rules

Attack suite: 90 runs, 9 attacks × 5 setups. Token flooding stopped only by the code layer.

Red team: 12 new attacks, 0 succeeded.

Data guide: invented figures are sent back by a number guard.
